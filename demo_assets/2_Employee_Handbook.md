## 1. Welcome to FinCorp

FinCorp is a financial advisory, asset management, and compliance consulting firm
headquartered in New York with a regional office in Singapore. Founded in 2014,
we serve over 80 enterprise clients globally. This handbook summarizes the
policies every employee is expected to follow.

## 2. Working Hours and Remote Work

- Standard working hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.
- Hybrid policy: all employees are expected **in office at least 3 days per week**.
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local.
- Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head
  and from Sarah Johnson (COO).

## 3. Paid Time Off

| Tenure | Annual vacation days | Sick days |
|--------|---------------------:|----------:|
| 0–2 years | 18 | 10 |
| 3–5 years | 22 | 10 |
| 6+ years | 28 | 12 |

- Unused vacation may be carried over up to a **maximum of 5 days** into the next
  calendar year.
- Public holidays follow the local jurisdiction calendar.

## 4. Compensation and Benefits

- Salaries are reviewed annually, effective April 1 each year.
- Performance bonuses are paid quarterly, based on individual and company targets.
- Health insurance is fully employer-funded for the employee; dependents covered
  at 70%.
- Retirement contribution: employer matches up to 6% of base salary.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement

Expense reimbursement follows the **FinCorp Expense Policy** (see separate document).
Any expense above policy limits requires written pre-approval from the
employee's department head. Overages above $5,000 require approval from
**Sarah Johnson (COO)**.

## 6. Confidentiality

All employees sign an NDA on day one. Client data, financial figures, and
internal strategy documents are **strictly confidential** and classified per
the FinCorp Data Classification Policy (see Compliance Guidelines).

Violations can result in immediate termination and legal action.

## 7. Code of Conduct

FinCorp expects all employees to:

- Act with integrity toward clients, colleagues, and regulators.
- Report any suspected fraud, misconduct, or harassment to the HR Director
  (Rachel Garcia) or via the anonymous ethics hotline: ethics@fincorp.com.
- Comply with the Compliance Guidelines, including insider trading restrictions.

## 8. Termination

- **Notice period:** 30 days for standard employees, 60 days for senior managers,
  90 days for the executive team.
- Termination for cause (fraud, gross misconduct, NDA violation) is immediate.
- Exit interviews are conducted by Rachel Garcia within 5 business days of
  the notice.

## 9. Grievances

Any grievance should first be raised with the employee's direct manager.
If unresolved within 10 business days, it is escalated to Rachel Garcia.
Cases involving the HR Director are escalated directly to Sarah Johnson (COO).
