# FinCorp — Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Version:** 4.0
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Sarah Johnson, Chief Operating Officer
**Finance contact:** John Smith, Chief Financial Officer
**Classification:** INTERNAL

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs all business expenses incurred by FinCorp employees and
their subsequent reimbursement. It applies to all employees worldwide.

## 2. General Rules

- All expenses must be **business-related, reasonable, and properly documented**.
- Receipts are mandatory for any expense of **$25 or more**.
- Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred.
- Reimbursements are paid on the 15th of each month for approved reports
  submitted by the 5th.
- Personal expenses, entertainment unrelated to clients, and fines are
  **never reimbursable**.

## 3. Expense Categories and Limits

### 3.1 Travel

| Item | Limit | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Domestic flights | Economy up to $800 | Business class requires VP+ approval |
| International flights | Economy up to $2,500 | Business class for flights > 8 hours |
| Hotels | $250/night (US), $300/night (EU), $200/night (SG) | |
| Rental car | $80/day | Mid-size; upgrade requires justification |
| Taxi / rideshare | Actual cost | Receipts mandatory |

### 3.2 Meals

| Item | Limit |
|------|------:|
| Breakfast | $25/person |
| Lunch | $40/person |
| Dinner | $75/person |
| Client entertainment dinner | $150/person |

### 3.3 Other

| Item | Limit |
|------|------:|
| Professional development / training | $2,500/year per employee |
| Home office equipment (remote workers) | $1,000/year per employee |
| Phone & internet stipend (remote workers) | $100/month |
| Conference attendance | $3,000/event (incl. ticket, travel, lodging) |

## 4. Approval Workflow

| Expense amount | Required approver |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $501 – $2,000 | Department head |
| $2,001 – $5,000 | CFO (John Smith) |
| **Above $5,000** | **COO (Sarah Johnson)** |
| Above $25,000 | COO **and** CFO jointly |

### Overages above policy limit

Any expense exceeding the per-category limit (e.g., a $275/night hotel in New York)
requires **written pre-approval from Sarah Johnson (COO)** before the expense
is incurred. Post-hoc approval of overages is not permitted except for
documented emergencies.

## 5. Corporate Credit Cards

- Issued to employees whose role requires frequent travel or client entertainment.
- Monthly reconciliation mandatory; receipts uploaded to the expense system.
- Failure to reconcile within 45 days suspends the card.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Items

- Alcohol, except during client entertainment (and within meal limits).
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, toll violations.
- Personal services (spa, laundry for short trips under 4 nights).
- Upgrades at personal cost (first class, premium hotel rooms) beyond policy.
- Family or partner travel.

## 7. Escalation and Disputes

Reimbursement disputes are handled by the Finance department. Unresolved
cases are escalated to Sarah Johnson (COO). Repeated policy violations are
reported to HR (Rachel Garcia) and may lead to disciplinary action.
