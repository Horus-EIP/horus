## 1. Regulatory Scope

FinCorp operates under the following regulatory regimes:

- **United States:** SEC registration, FINRA membership, SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley).
- **European Union:** GDPR for personal data; **MiCA** (Markets in Crypto-Assets)
  entering enforcement June 30, 2026.
- **Singapore:** MAS licensing for advisory activities.

## 2. GDPR Obligations

As a data controller processing EU resident data, FinCorp must:

1. Maintain a Record of Processing Activities (ROPA). Owner: Michael Brown.
2. Respond to Data Subject Access Requests within **30 calendar days**.
3. Report personal data breaches to the supervisory authority within
   **72 hours** of discovery.
4. Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for any new system
   processing personal data at scale.
5. Maintain processor agreements with all third-party vendors handling
   personal data.

## 3. SOX Compliance

Required controls:

- Quarterly financial controls attestation by the CFO (John Smith).
- Annual external audit, cycle ending **December 31**. Next audit: Jan–Mar 2026.
- Segregation of duties in the finance function — no single person may
  both approve and record a transaction over **$10,000**.

## 4. Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

- KYC performed on all new clients prior to contract signing.
- Transactions above **$10,000** flagged for enhanced due diligence.
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR) filed within 30 days of detection.
- AML training mandatory for all client-facing staff, annually.

## 5. Insider Trading

- Employees in possession of material non-public information must not trade
  in affected securities.
- Trading window: open only **2 business days after each earnings release**,
  closing 14 days before the next close.
- Pre-clearance required from the Compliance Officer (Michael Brown) for all
  trades by executives and senior managers.

## 6. MiCA Readiness

The MiCA regulation enters enforcement on **June 30, 2026**. FinCorp's Singapore
crypto advisory line is in scope. A mitigation plan is due **March 1, 2026**,
owned by Michael Brown, reviewed by John Smith (CFO) and Sarah Johnson (COO).
