## 1. Executive Summary

FinCorp closed Q4 2025 with total revenue of **$4.2M**, up 12% year-over-year. Operating
expenses grew to **$3.1M**, leaving a net operating income of **$1.1M**. Overall 2025 full-year
revenue reached **$15.8M**, beating the board target of $15M by 5.3%.

The quarter was marked by the successful onboarding of three enterprise clients
(Acme Bank, Meridian Holdings, and Kestrel Capital) and the completion of our SOC 2
Type II audit on November 24, 2025.

## 5. Risks and Outlook

The primary risk flagged this quarter is regulatory: the upcoming **MiCA enforcement
in the EU on June 30, 2026** may affect our Singapore crypto advisory line. The
Compliance team, led by Michael Brown, is producing a mitigation plan due
**March 1, 2026**.

Outlook for Q1 2026: revenue target **$4.5M**, driven by a full quarter of Acme Bank
revenue and expansion of Meridian Holdings to advisory services.
