Duties of a plot holder
Every plot holder gives 12 volunteer hours per season. Hours are written on the board inside the shed. Each plot holder keeps the paths next to the plot clear and does not let weeds go to seed.

[garden-handbook.txt]
Compost
The shared compost bins stand behind the shed. They are turned and emptied every Saturday morning, so bring green waste before Friday evening. Cooked food, meat and diseased plants do not go in the bins.
