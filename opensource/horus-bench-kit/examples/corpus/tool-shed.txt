Fernhill Community Garden: tool shed

Hand tools
Spades, forks, rakes, hoes and watering cans are free to use. Put them back clean before you leave.

Large tools
The rotary tiller and the hedge trimmer are lent on booking with the shed coordinator. The rotary tiller needs a $25 deposit, paid back when the tiller comes back clean and working. A member can keep the tiller for two days at most.

Opening of the shed
The shed is open whenever the garden gate is open. Members put the shed key back in the key box by the door.
