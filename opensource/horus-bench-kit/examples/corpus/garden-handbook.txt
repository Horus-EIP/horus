Fernhill Community Garden: member handbook

Opening hours
On weekdays the gate opens at 7:00 am and closes at 8:30 pm. At weekends it opens at 8:00 am and closes at sunset. The last member to leave locks the gate with the code given at the spring meeting.

Duties of a plot holder
Every plot holder gives 12 volunteer hours per season. Hours are written on the board inside the shed. Each plot holder keeps the paths next to the plot clear and does not let weeds go to seed.

Compost
The shared compost bins stand behind the shed. They are turned and emptied every Saturday morning, so bring green waste before Friday evening. Cooked food, meat and diseased plants do not go in the bins.

Who to ask
The garden coordinator, Marta Lindqvist, handles new members and the waiting list. The shed coordinator, Ines Okafor, looks after the tool shed and lends the large tools.
