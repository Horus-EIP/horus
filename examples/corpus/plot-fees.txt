Fernhill Community Garden: plot fees for the season

Plot sizes and fees
Half plot (3 m by 3 m): $45 per season
Full plot (6 m by 3 m): $80 per season
Raised bed for members with limited mobility: $20 per season

Early payment
Fees paid before 1 March get a 10% discount. Fees are paid by bank transfer, or in cash at the spring meeting.

What the fee covers
Water from the garden taps, a share of the compost and the use of the hand tools are included in the fee.

Leaving the garden
A member who gives up a plot before 1 May gets half of the fee back. After 1 May the fee is not refunded.
