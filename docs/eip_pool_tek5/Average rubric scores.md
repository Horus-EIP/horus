## Feedback summary

### Horus

The strongest aspects were private, locally hosted AI, ease of installation, the interface, and the inclusion of a business and marketing model. Reviewers recognized a genuine need for companies that cannot send confidential data to external AI services.

The pitch was considered too long, dense and insufficiently engaging, with several comments about exceeding the time limit. The team should simplify the slides, sharpen the narrative and be transparent about hardware requirements, security, pricing and CPU performance. A more convincing end-user demonstration and clearer explanation of practical deployment would strengthen the proposal considerably.
